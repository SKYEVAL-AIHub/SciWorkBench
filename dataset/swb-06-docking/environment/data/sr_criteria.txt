Success Rate (SR) Criteria

The SR is defined as the proportion of molecules that satisfy the following nine physicochemical property constraints:

C1: Vina dock < -8.18 kcal/mol
C2: QED > 0.25
C3: SA > 0.59
C4: -0.4 <= LogP <= 5.6
C5: TPSA <= 140 square angstroms
C6: Fsp3 >= 0.42
C7: HBA <= 10
C8: HBD <= 5
C9: ROTB <= 10

A molecule is considered to have passed the SR filter if and only if it satisfies all nine conditions simultaneously. For cases where a condition cannot be evaluated due to missing data, the molecule's SR status should be reported as indeterminate for that condition.
